# IQON image provenance — September 2026

All 14 primary product images were regenerated with the built-in image tool from the repository’s supplied IQON packshots. Packaging identity, colors, container format and original artwork were the reference; the change standardizes studio lighting, backdrop, camera angle and shadow. NMN and Resveratrol also have new matching gallery detail images. Source images remain in git history. Exact prompts are in `studio-v4-prompts.json` and `studio-v4-detail-prompts.json`.

The coastal, skincare and Pilates campaign photographs are AI-generated editorial imagery. Exact prompts are in `campaign-v4-prompts.json`. They do not depict identified customers, reviewers, clinicians or endorsers. The earlier daily-water, everyday-movement and skin-ritual images are also generated editorial images recovered from the prior IQON design work.

No generated person is presented as a customer testimonial or a product result. `lib/reviews.ts` is intentionally empty until genuine, permissioned IQON supplement/skincare reviews are available.

WebP files use encoding-only compression; packaging text, background and shadows were not composited or redrawn after generation. Original-label wording on rendered packaging is inherited reference artwork, not independent product substantiation.
