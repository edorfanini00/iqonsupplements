# IQON store redesign — reference study

The first draft was rejected as sparse, presentation-like, and typographically off brand. This revision makes catalog browsing and shopping primary.

## Sources inspected

- Timeline homepage, supplement shop and Mitopure Softgels product page: https://www.timeline.com/ ; https://www.timeline.com/shop ; https://www.timeline.com/products/mitopure-softgels-vegan
- Timeline skincare shop and Firming Serum product page: https://www.timeline.com/skincare/shop ; https://www.timeline.com/skincare/products/firming-serum
- Skye Tides homepage, full collection, and GLOW product page: https://skyetides.com/ ; https://skyetides.com/collections/all ; https://skyetides.com/products/glow
- Seed homepage and DS-01 page: https://seed.com/ ; https://seed.com/daily-synbiotic
- Augustinus Bader homepage and The Rich Cream page: https://augustinusbader.com/eu/en/the-rich-cream

## Application

Timeline: strong separation of supplements and skincare; category/concern navigation; layered product galleries; purchase frequency choices; comparisons; product information below the first viewport; related routines.

Skye Tides: cohesive polished packshots, tactile formula images and human content; gallery thumbnails; clear purchase controls; routine merchandising; sticky add-to-cart and detailed FAQ.

Seed: recognizable product family, strong format/ingredient storytelling, clear purchase summary, editorial product-in-hand photography.

Augustinus Bader: retail depth by category and routine, size options and discovery sets, refined product hierarchy.

IQON direction: charcoal, silver and cool gray with ivory/sage on labels; distinctive uppercase serif wordmark; restrained sans supporting typography, editorial serif headings at a practical store scale. No oversized thin Inter slogans.

Do not import competitors’ imagery, claims, metrics, endorsements, reviews, certifications or policies. Provisional IQON assortment and sample prices are explicitly identified as such; checkout does not accept money or submit orders.
