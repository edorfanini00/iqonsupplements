# IQON launch pricing proposal

Research date: **10 September 2026**. Market: **United States, USD, direct-to-consumer**. Prices below exclude tax and shipping. These are proposed prices for review, not an instruction to publish products or activate discounts.

## Recommendation

Position IQON as a premium, accessible daily-routine brand. Use **$24–$49 for supplements** and **$28–$58 for skincare**. Lead with creatine, collagen and a simple skincare routine. The packaging can support a modest premium; the evidence available does not support charging more than established specialist brands across the entire collection.

The skincare prices currently in the website preview—$88 serum, $72 cream and $38 cleanser—are too ambitious for formulas still described as concepts in the catalog. My recommended $58 / $48 / $28 ladder is provisional until the manufacturer confirms the formulas and costs.

## Why each recommendation makes sense

1. **Creatine — $29.** Momentous works out to $9.55 per 100 g; IQON at $29 is $10.32 per 100 g. Its 281 g equivalent would cost $26.84. IQON therefore asks roughly an 8% premium per gram, already enough for the brand at launch. The mathematical 5 g comparison gives 56.2 servings and about $0.52 each; this is not a verified IQON scoop size or label serving claim.
2. **Unflavored collagen — $32.** The closest Vital Proteins pack is $27; the Advanced formula is $29.99. IQON’s 280 g jar can sit slightly above these, but $45–$55 would overreach for this quantity. The powder is bovine collagen; label scoop weight still needs confirmation, so no invented “30-day supply” claim.
3. **Chocolate collagen — $36.** The closest Vital Proteins 13.5 oz pack is approximately 383 g at $27. IQON asks about a third more for a similar total pack weight. That is a meaningful premium already. The 378 g contains cocoa and other ingredients, so do not compare total grams as if all were collagen. Test $32 if the $36 price materially reduces conversion.
4. **Colostrum — $49.** This creates an accessible premium anchor near ARMRA’s $43.99 travel pack. IQON lists 30 × 2.3 g servings with 25% IgG, mathematically 575 mg IgG per stated serving. Compare the actual panel, not just jar appearance. ARMRA’s page recommends multiple servings a day; 30 servings is not automatically a month for that brand. I would not charge $79–$99 for this 69 g IQON jar.
5. **Colon Gentle Cleanse — $29.** Sachet convenience supports a moderate premium, but Sunfiber offers 30 sticks for $34.99 with a clearly disclosed 6 g soluble fiber per serving. IQON’s sachet is 3 g of total mixed ingredients, not 3 g of fiber. At 1–2 sachets daily the pack lasts 15–30 days, or $0.97–$1.93 per day at the proposed price.
6. **Keto-5 — $24.** Its caffeine and botanical blend belongs near mainstream weight-management supplements, not premium exogenous ketones. Hydroxycut’s listed $19.99 is a category anchor, with a different formula and availability limitation. Individual IQON active doses are missing. Keep this an inexpensive secondary product rather than the core premium-brand story.
7. **GLP-1 Support — $44.** Codeage’s $39.99 companion product is a useful positioning anchor. IQON’s vitamin, mineral and digestive-support blend can justify a modest premium if the final panel and cost support it. This is a supplement; do not benchmark its value against prescription GLP-1 medicines or imply equivalent effects. Two capsules daily make the current 60-capsule pack 30 days: $1.47 daily.
8. **Liver Support — $29.** Slightly below Gaia’s $31.49 60-count bottle is appropriate for a new brand without equally established proof or disclosed individual botanical quantities. IQON’s two-capsule directions imply 30 days, approximately $0.97 daily. Gaia’s capsule count alone is not evidence of an equal daily dose or duration.
9. **NMN — $49.** ProHealth’s current 500 mg × 30-serving product is $42.95; IQON would be about 14% higher. This is the most defensible upper-end supplement price in the proposed collection, contingent on verified identity, purity and supply specifications. At one capsule daily, $1.63 per day. A supplier’s purity statement is not an independently reviewed batch certificate.
10. **Resveratrol — $24.** IQON describes 600 mg of a 50%-standardized root complex, which corresponds to 300 mg trans-resveratrol for that stated amount. The available record does not clearly establish whether 600 mg is per capsule or per two-capsule serving. ProHealth offers a stated 1,000 mg trans-resveratrol serving at $31.08. Until the panel is clarified, IQON should be an accessible companion product, not a higher-priced potency leader.
11. **Hair, Skin & Nails Gummies — $24.** HUM’s $26 product puts a clear ceiling on an everyday gummy without stronger differentiation. IQON is a two-gummy, 30-day pack at $0.80 daily. Its fish collagen also makes it different from HUM’s vegan product; avoid assuming equal ingredient amounts or calling IQON vegan.
12. **Peptide Serum — $58.** This sits between Naturium’s $25 and Medik8’s $68, leaning toward the premium end. It requires a credible finished formula and good packaging. If the eventual formula is basic, launch closer to $48 instead. Do not treat a concept render or ingredient-category research as evidence of product-specific skin results.
13. **Barrier Cream — $48.** Between Rhode’s $32 and Skinfix’s $54. At 50 mL this supports the IQON presentation while leaving room below an established premium specialist. A basic moisturizer formula would justify the lower $42 test point rather than $48.
14. **Gentle Cleanser — $28.** Near Medik8’s $30, well above Geek & Gorgeous’s $9.90. Cleansers are an accessible entry into the brand, so the current $38 preview price creates unnecessary friction. Sell the routine through a bundle instead of forcing premium margin on the cleanser alone.

## Discounts, bundles and delivery

Recommend a straightforward **15% ongoing subscription discount** for appropriate replenishment products after real Shopify selling plans are implemented. Do not present a first-order promotion as the permanent price. For comparison, Momentous shows a 25% introductory subscription offer but 10% on subsequent orders; Vital Proteins’ ordinary subscription offer is 15%, and ProHealth NMN lists $38.66 against $42.95 one-time. Those are different commercial offers.

Suggested bundles, subject to landed-cost review:

| Bundle | Separate prices total | Proposed bundle price | Savings |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Creatine + unflavored collagen | $61 | **$55** | $6 / 9.8% |
| NMN + resveratrol | $73 | **$65** | $8 / 11.0% |
| Cleanser + serum + cream | $134 | **$119** | $15 / 11.2% |

Do not stack the full subscription discount on these bundle prices by default. Bundle recommendations are merchandising proposals, not a claim that combined products have been tested together. Start with ordinary prices, not permanent “sales” or invented crossed-out reference prices.

**Shipping hypothesis:** free standard US shipping from **$75 after discounts**, with **$5.95 below $75**. This is a proposal to check against fulfillment quotes, not a configured rate or a promise that it is profitable. The website currently says 3–5 business days; obtain a fulfillment commitment that supports that statement before launch. Confirm whether the timeframe includes processing.

## Margin decision before publishing

Actual product costs, label/packaging charges, pick-and-pack, postage, taxes/duties and acquisition costs have not been supplied. Public Supliful listings closely match several IQON specifications, including [GLP-1 Support](https://supliful.com/catalog/glp-1-support-capsules), [NMN](https://supliful.com/catalog/nmn-capsules) and [gummies](https://supliful.com/catalog/hair-skin-nails-gummies), but supplier prices require login. This is evidence of matching specifications, not confirmation of IQON’s supplier agreement or costs.

Use **70% product gross margin after a 15% discount** as a planning target, not a verified result or an industry rule. Maximum landed product COGS at that target is:

`retail price × 0.85 × 0.30`

| Retail price | Discounted revenue | Maximum landed product COGS at 70% product gross margin |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $24 | $20.40 | $6.12 |
| $28 | $23.80 | $7.14 |
| $29 | $24.65 | $7.40 |
| $32 | $27.20 | $8.16 |
| $36 | $30.60 | $9.18 |
| $44 | $37.40 | $11.22 |
| $48 | $40.80 | $12.24 |
| $49 | $41.65 | $12.50 |
| $58 | $49.30 | $14.79 |

Rounded ceilings are guidance, not negotiated supplier prices. For Keto-5 and cleanse, these discounted amounts are only stress tests; their subscriptions are on hold. Product gross margin here excludes fulfillment, outbound shipping, payment fees, returns and customer acquisition.

Contribution per order should be calculated separately:

`net merchandise revenue + shipping collected − landed COGS − fulfillment − postage − payment fees − expected returns − acquisition cost`

For illustration only: a $75 basket discounted 15% produces $63.75 merchandise revenue. Assuming $20 landed COGS, $3 fulfillment, $6 postage and 2.9% + $0.30 payment processing on $63.75, contribution before acquisition is about $32.60. After an assumed $15 acquisition cost it is about $17.60. Actual taxable totals, card types, shipping charges and returns change this result.

If quoted costs exceed the ceiling, reduce the discount, reconsider the SKU or negotiate supply before increasing prices beyond the competitive range. The two weakest pricing cases are Keto-5 and the not-yet-final skincare formulas; the most important missing specification is the resveratrol dose panel.

## Shopify commercial setup

[Shopify’s US pricing page](https://www.shopify.com/pricing) lists Basic at **$39/month**, or **$29/month with yearly billing**. Standard online card rates start at **2.9% + $0.30**; premium cards are listed at **3.5% + $0.30**, with additional international charges. Third-party payment providers can add a Shopify transaction fee. Use the actual merchant offer and card mix in the final model.

Basic is the starting recommendation for this launch; the research does not establish a need for Plus. The connected store is a trial. No paid plan, payment provider, bank information, subscription app, shipping rate or tax setting has been activated through this work. Draft catalog prices remain proposals until approved.
