# IQON product gallery direction

The approved IQON product pack shots are the photographic anchor. Preserve the
original first image, label design, logo, product color, and proportions.

Each gallery has four distinct roles:

1. Original approved packaging image, without overlaid copy.
2. One clear formula fact, using exact native typography and label data.
3. A separate IQON product photograph that shows the physical format or packaging.
4. A product-specific guide to preparation, serving format, ingredient roles, or use.

Use the existing charcoal, silver, and cool neutral palette. Keep photography and
information separate. Equal 4:5 frames, an 8 px desktop gutter, and short headlines
make the sequence consistent; the content and diagram differ by product.

Do not fill gallery slots with generated people, older hands, decorative food,
roots, or generic material macros. Do not place generic lifestyle portraits in
these product galleries. The rejected pilot portraits were not published.

The formula and use graphics are native HTML/SVG, not generated photographs.
They remain sharp, selectable, and accessible when enlarged. Their quantities
come from `supplement-details.ts`, `skincare-range.json`, and the existing supplied
product content. No competitor study numbers, invented clinical outcomes,
before-and-after images, or testimonial claims are used.

Timeline's product pages informed the separation of packaging, information, and
use content. IQON retains its own packaging, palette, type, and product statements.

Current guide copy and product photo selection: `lib/product-gallery-content.ts`.
Homepage campaign photography is unchanged.
