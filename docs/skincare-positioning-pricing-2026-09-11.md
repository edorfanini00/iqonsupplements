# IQON skincare: positioning and launch pricing

Research date: September 11, 2026. Market: United States, USD, before sales tax and shipping. These are launch recommendations applied to the website catalog and Shopify drafts, not experimentally proven optimal prices.

## Recommendation

Position IQON as accessible premium skincare: considered formulas, restrained packaging, and clear choices for an everyday routine. Lead with hydration, brightness and smoother-looking texture. Let the silver packaging and consistent photography support the premium experience; do not rely on unsupported clinical claims to justify price.

The competitive space spans ingredient-led value brands such as Naturium and Timeless, and established professional brands such as Dermalogica. IQON can sit between them, but cannot assume the trust or pricing power of an established clinical brand. The proposed customer is someone who wants a polished, understandable routine and is willing to pay more for that experience. This audience definition is a positioning hypothesis, not a finding from customer interviews.

Several public supplier listings already exceed these ceilings. Therefore these prices support an accessible-premium launch test, not a promise that aggressive paid acquisition will be profitable. The $48 pads are the clearest example: a 15% discount reduces revenue to $40.80 and listing-based gross margin to 41.2%, before other costs. Do not enable a blanket 15% skincare subscription discount.

If confirmed costs resemble the public listings and paid acquisition is essential, negotiate costs or test higher tickets before scaling. At a $24 product cost, pads require $60 just to reach 60% product gross margin; at $29.50, retinol requires $73.75. Raising all prices immediately would improve arithmetic but leave IQON closer to established brands without established demand. Start with the applied ladder, control promotions and measure contribution per order.

## Merchandising and product positioning

Lead the collection with the cleanser, then tonic, pads, vitamin C, retinol, eye gel and moisturizer. Feature Hydra C and Copper Peptide Restore Cream in treatment-focused messaging. Make individual goals easy to find: cleanse, hydrate, refine, brighten, renew, eye care and moisturize.

The range is a menu, not a mandatory seven-step routine. The cleanser and moisturizer total $104; adding Hydra C totals $172. Those totals are price architecture examples, not instructions to layer every active. Keep toner and eye care optional. Do not present the exfoliating pads, Retinol Rx and copper cream as a universal combined evening regimen: the supplier's copper cream also includes retinol.

Use the supplied images unchanged, with the full package visible. Use quiet, specific copy describing texture, format and appearance benefits. Avoid unsupported percentages of improvement, fabricated reviews, “medical grade,” prescription implications, and IQON-specific clinical results. Do not describe these formulas as fragrance-free or universally suitable for sensitive skin without verification.

## Implementation and launch measurement

Seven Shopify drafts replace the three placeholder skincare records, which were archived. Prices, images, sizes and product copy are added to the site. Unpublished skincare can be browsed with ordering disabled; once published to the storefront, Shopify remains authoritative for price, currency, variants and stock. No stock quantities were invented. No subscription or compare-at discount was activated.

Before accepting orders, confirm the exact supplier variants, landed costs and available inventory, then publish the products to the storefront sales channel. Review whether the final label matches the descriptions and directions. Payment setup was reported complete by the merchant; a complete checkout and fulfillment test remains separate.

After launch, track product-page-to-cart rate, checkout completion, average order value, contribution after fulfillment and acquisition, and repeat purchase by product. Compare results across similar traffic sources. Test one price or offer change at a time and choose sample sizes based on actual traffic; an arbitrary short test is not proof. Revisit pads and retinol first if margin is weak, and Hydra C first if customers choose cheaper alternatives despite engaging with the page.
